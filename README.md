a. What problem does your app solve or what purpose does it serve?

reviving my previous hackathon Idea: The Problem: Addressing Food Insecurity and Resource Waste on Campus
1. Underutilized Food Resources -Clubs and Organizations frequently host events with leftover food that needs a better outreach system to reach students who could use it. -Local Businesses often have surplus food that goes to waste instead of being redistributed.
2. Inefficient Dining Dollar Sharing Many students run out of dining dollars weeks before the semester ends, while others have a surplus that ultimately goes to waste.
b. Who is the intended user?
Flexible Accounts: Users can sign up as an Individual, a Club/Organization, or a Local Business

c. What actions will users be able to perform (e.g., sign up, add items, search,
comment, etc.)?
Anyone can create a post detailing available food, including the location and a custom message describing what's available (e.g., "Leftover pizza from 6 PM event," "Box of pastries available until 1 PM").
